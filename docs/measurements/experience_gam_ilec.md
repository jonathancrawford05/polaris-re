## Caveats

- 277,173 cells with uw_class == 'U' (underwriting class *unknown*, not *not applicable*) were held out of this class-conditioned fit. 'NA' rows are retained as their own stratum. Pass keep_unknown_uw_class=True to include them.
- 2 cells had zero exposure and were dropped — a Poisson offset needs exposure * q_base > 0, and a cell nobody was exposed in carries no information. They held 0.0 deaths, as expected.
- Cells are pooled across duration, so select-period mortality is absorbed into the attained-age effect. If the duration mix drifts with calendar year, part of that drift lands in the fitted improvement. Pass group_keys including 'duration_months' to separate them, at ~60x the cell count.
- Pearson dispersion φ = 2.25, so the quasi-Poisson covariance scaling was applied: every band below is 1.50x wider than a plain-Poisson fit would have reported. The point estimates are unchanged — scaling touches the covariance only. A φ this far above 1 means unmodelled heterogeneity, which is expected on real grouped data and is why the scaling is not optional here.
- The base offset is estimated from these same cells, so `overall_ae` is ~1 by construction and is not an independent check on the level. The fitted improvement is unaffected: it is a calendar contrast, and every calendar-invariant term — the offset included — cancels in the difference.
